  4. Jun 2024 – May 2026

    M.Tech, Catalysis Technology, IIT Madras

    Department of Chemical Engineering · CGPA 8.51/10

    Two years on how catalysts work and how to make and test them: adsorption and catalysis, solids and surfaces, reactor theory, thermodynamics and transport, a catalyst preparation and characterisation lab, and computational methods in catalysis.

    Thesis
    Formic acid can power small fuel cells, but the platinum catalyst that oxidises it gets clogged by carbon monoxide from a side reaction, and the current fades within an hour. With Prof. Raghuram Chetty I tested two remedies: a sub-monolayer of bismuth on commercial Pt/C, which leaves fewer of the neighbouring platinum sites that CO needs to form, and a dissolved quinone (AQSA) that acts as a redox mediator. In three-electrode half-cell tests the catalyst with the least bismuth worked best, keeping 92.9% of its current over a one-hour hold against 58.5% for unmodified Pt/C. Read more
