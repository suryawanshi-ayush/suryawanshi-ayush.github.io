Keeping platinum clean in formic acid fuel cells

For my M.Tech thesis I modified commercial Pt/C with small amounts of bismuth to resist carbon monoxide poisoning during formic acid oxidation, and tested a quinone mediator. The catalyst with the least bismuth held its current best; the mediator lowered one-hour retention.
